# Demo credits

The live demo's costume party mode has four special guests. They are fan tributes, used with permission, and they live only on the demo page, in [tributes.js](tributes.js):

- **jacksepticeye**: a tribute to Jacksepticeye and his green eyeball character.
- **vanoss**: a tribute to VanossGaming and his owl.
- **markiplier**: a tribute to Markiplier: the black shirt, the hair and the pink mustache.
